Training & Standards

Vetted, Trained, Accountable

Every manager, patrolling officer and guard at Kingsman Security is vetted and trained before deployment — and continues to be assessed afterward.

Vigilant • Honour • Secure

KDN Security Vetting

All managers, patrolling officers and guards undergo KDN (Ministry of Home Affairs) security vetting before employment.

Certified Security Guard (CSG) Training

Personnel attend the CSG training programme, customised by KDN, PDRM and PIKM.

Ongoing Assessment

Regular assessment, training and orientation continues after initial deployment, not just at onboarding.

Licensing

KDN License No. 02250737

Kingsman Security Sdn Bhd operates under KDN License No. 02250737, the statutory license issued by Malaysia’s Ministry of Home Affairs required to operate a security firm. Where armed guard deployments are provided, personnel carry PDRM-licensed firearms as a stated, regulated operational capability — presented here as a business credential, not a marketing claim.

Kingsman Security is also a member of Persatuan Industri Kawalan Keselamatan Malaysia (PIKM), the Malaysian Security Industry Association.
