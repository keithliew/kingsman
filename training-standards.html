Training & Standards

Vetted, Trained, Accountable

Every manager, patrolling officer and guard at Kingsman Security is vetted and trained before deployment — and continues to be assessed afterward.

Vigilant • Honour • Secure

KDN Security Vetting

All managers, patrolling officers and guards undergo KDN (Ministry of Home Affairs) security vetting before employment.

Certified Security Guard (CSG) Training

Personnel attend the CSG training programme, customised by KDN, PDRM and PIKM.

Ongoing Assessment

After completing CSG, personnel go through regular internal assessment, training and orientation programmes to understand each client’s SOPs and expectations.

Licensing

KDN License No. 02250737

Kingsman Security Services Sdn Bhd operates under KDN License No. 02250737, the statutory license issued by Malaysia’s Ministry of Home Affairs required to operate a security firm. We maintain full compliance with industry regulations and operational standards.

Kingsman Security is also a member of Persatuan Institusi Kawalan Malaysia (PIKM), the Malaysia Institute of Security Control.
