Who We Are

Protecting People, Property & Assets

Kingsman Security Services Sdn Bhd was established in 2024 to provide professional, reliable, and comprehensive security solutions across Malaysia. Founded by Dato’ Narander Singh A/L Chand Singh, the company is licensed by the Ministry of Home Affairs (KDN License No. 02250737) and is a member of Persatuan Institusi Kawalan Malaysia (PIKM).

We specialise in Manned Guarding, Executive Protection, CCTV Monitoring & Surveillance, Access Control Management, Patrol Services, and Special Security Assignments. Guided by our core values of Vigilant, Honour, Secure, we are committed to protecting people, property, and assets with professionalism and excellence.

Leadership & Employee Commitment

We strongly believe in good leadership qualities and the latest practical management practices. With this in mind, Kingsman Security has invested in selecting and securing capable security managers, reliable security guards — both local and from Nepal — bodyguards, and armed guards. Our commitment to our employees is to provide an environment that promotes self-development and opportunities for career advancement.

Expertise & Client Relations

Our managers, patrolling officers and guards go through proper KDN security vetting before employment and complete the Certified Security Guard (CSG) training programme customised by KDN, PDRM and PIKM. After completing CSG, they also go through regular internal assessment, training and orientation programmes to understand each client’s SOPs and expectations.

Leadership

Led By Experienced Professionals

Our leadership team brings decades of experience from the Malaysian Prison Department, the Royal Malaysian Air Force and private-sector security operations.

Chairman

Dato’ Narander Singh A/L Chand Singh

D.S.I.S, A.M.S, K.M.N, A.M.P, P.J.K

Dato’ Narander Singh brings more than 36 years of experience in the Malaysian Prison Department (Jabatan Penjara Malaysia), where he held senior leadership positions including Director of Sungai Buloh Prison, Kajang Prison and Taiping Prison, as well as Commandant of the Malaysian Prison Academy.

He holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), a Bachelor of Arts (Hons) in Psychology, a Diploma in Management Science, and professional certifications in Advanced Prison Management (United Kingdom) and Correctional Administration & Leadership (Thailand). With expertise in security operations, institutional leadership, policy development and professional training, he provides strategic leadership and governance to the company.

Director

En. Jeffari Bin Abdul Malek

BAT, PJK (Kedah), PPS

With over 26 years of service in the Royal Malaysian Air Force (RMAF), Warrant Officer 1 (Retired) Jeffari Bin Abdul Malek brings extensive leadership, operational, and personnel management experience to the company.

Having served as an instructor, Regional Headquarters Warrant Officer, Education Headquarters Warrant Officer, and Senior Enlisted Advisor to the Chief of Air Force, he possesses exceptional expertise in leadership, discipline, training, and organisational management. His leadership philosophy emphasises leading by example, fostering teamwork, developing people through mentorship, and maintaining the highest standards of operational excellence.

Operations Director

Dr. Kailash Menon A/L Ramachenderan

10+ years in security operations

Dr. Kailash Menon is a seasoned security professional with over 10 years of experience in security operations, workforce management, risk assessment, and client relations. Holding a Diploma in Automotive Engineering, he has successfully led security teams across various sectors while maintaining high standards of professionalism, discipline, and service excellence.

As Operations Director, he oversees the company’s daily operations, manpower management, service delivery, and strategic planning, and plays a key role in driving client satisfaction and organisational growth.
