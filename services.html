Our Services

Security Solutions Built Around Six Core Pillars

Every engagement is drawn from these primary capabilities, supported by a set of additional specialist services below.

Vigilant • Honour • Secure
01

Manned Guarding

Static and mobile guard deployment for commercial, residential and industrial premises, delivered by personnel who have undergone KDN security vetting and Certified Security Guard (CSG) training.

[TO BE CONFIRMED] Source material lists a service titled “Unarmed Guard Services” whose description also states guards carry PDRM-licensed firearms (pistol & pump gun). Pending client clarification, we present unarmed and armed guarding as two distinct offerings below rather than one contradictory service. Please confirm before this page goes live.

Unarmed Guard Services

Uniformed guards for general access control, reception, and premises protection duties.

Armed Guard Services

PDRM-licensed armed guards for higher-risk deployments, stated here as a factual operational capability under Malaysia’s firearms licensing framework — not a marketing claim.

02

Executive Protection & Close Protection

Personal protection for executives, VIPs and high-profile individuals, referred to in source material as “Bodyguard” services. Personnel are drawn from the same KDN-vetted, CSG-trained pool as our guarding staff.

03

CCTV Monitoring & Surveillance

Continuous camera surveillance and monitoring of premises and assets, supporting both real-time observation and incident review.

04

Access Control Management

Managed entry systems — including keycard access — to regulate and monitor who enters your premises.

05

Patrol Services

Scheduled and randomised mobile patrols across your property or route, deterring intrusion and confirming premises security between static checkpoints.

06

Special Security Assignments

Crowd management, access control, VIP protection and emergency preparedness for events and one-off assignments.

Additional Capabilities

Specialist & Supporting Services

Beyond the six core pillars, Kingsman Security offers the following specialist capabilities.

Cash-in-Transit & Cargo Escort

Armed escorts for secure cash transfers to the bank, and cargo escort services by road from point to point.

Guard Dog Service

German Shepherd, Rottweiler and Doberman patrol/guard dogs, handled by trained personnel.

Central Monitoring System (CMS)

24/7 sensor and alarm monitoring for fire and break-in events, with immediate alerting to the owner and relevant authorities.

Emergency Response Team

Rapid response and patrol units providing incident response and coordination with the relevant authorities.

Residential, Commercial & Companies Security

A positioning line covering condominiums, homes and businesses — delivered through the core pillars above, not a distinct operational service.
