Our Services

Security Solutions Built Around Six Core Pillars

Every engagement is drawn from these primary capabilities, supported by a set of additional specialist services below.

Vigilant • Honour • Secure
01

Manned Guarding

Static and mobile guard deployment for commercial, residential and industrial premises, delivered by personnel who have undergone KDN security vetting and Certified Security Guard (CSG) training.

Unarmed Guard Services

To assure your safety and security, we provide both local and Nepali guards who secure your premises and deter any unauthorised entry. Our guards have 24/7 access to a back-up team for immediate assistance in the event of an emergency.

Armed Guard Services

Armed guards for higher-risk deployments, including cash-in-transit and cargo escort assignments.

Residential, Commercial & Companies Security

Tailored security solutions for homes, businesses, and commercial properties, including manned guarding, access control, and routine inspection to prevent risks and respond swiftly.

02

Executive Protection & Close Protection

Our professional bodyguards provide close protection for individuals, executives, and VIPs, ensuring their safety in every situation. Personnel are drawn from the same KDN-vetted, CSG-trained pool as our guarding staff.

03

CCTV Monitoring & Surveillance

Our surveillance solutions provide 24/7 real-time monitoring and recording to detect, deter, and respond to incidents. High-definition CCTV systems are installed strategically and monitored by our trained operators.

04

Access Control Management

We implement advanced access control systems to manage and monitor entry points, ensuring authorised access only. Our solutions include keycard systems, biometric access, visitor management, and integrated security platforms.

05

Patrol Services

Dedicated patrolling across your property or route, deterring intrusion and confirming premises security between static checkpoints. Our patrol units also form part of our Emergency Response Team, ready to respond to incidents at any time.

06

Special Security Assignments

We provide professional security for events, gatherings, and special assignments with precision and discretion. Our team is experienced in crowd management, access control, VIP protection, and emergency preparedness.

Additional Capabilities

Specialist & Supporting Services

Beyond the six core pillars, Kingsman Security offers the following specialist capabilities.

Cash-in-Transit & Cargo Escort

Armed escorts for secure cash transfers to the bank, and cargo escort services to transport valuable items by road from point to point. Our alert armed guards keep your staff and assets safe at every step.

K9 Guard Dog Service

Our well-trained dogs, such as German Shepherds, Rottweilers and Dobermans, strengthen your security system where necessary.

Central Monitoring System (CMS)

24/7 centralised security monitoring with real-time surveillance, rapid incident detection, alert management and coordinated response to ensure continuous protection of your people, property and assets.

Emergency Response Team

Our rapid response team and patrol units are always ready to respond to emergencies and ensure immediate action. We conduct mobile patrols, incident response, and coordination with relevant authorities for maximum safety.
