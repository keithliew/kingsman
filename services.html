Our Services

Comprehensive Security Solutions

Our services to meet growing security demands, delivered by KDN-vetted, CSG-trained personnel across Malaysia.

Vigilant • Honour • Secure
01

Unarmed Guard Services

To assure your safety and security, we provide both local and Nepali guards who secure your premises and deter any unauthorised entry. Our guards have 24/7 access to a back-up team for immediate assistance in the event of an emergency.

02

Cash-in-Transit & Cargo Escort

We provide armed escorts for secure cash transfers to the bank, and cargo escort services to transport valuable items by road from point to point. Our alert armed guards keep your staff and assets safe at every step.

03

Dedicated Patrolling

Dedicated mobile patrols of your premises by Kingsman officers in marked patrol vehicles, deterring intrusion and supporting your on-site guards.

04

K9 Guard Dog Services

Our well-trained dogs, such as German Shepherds, Rottweilers and Dobermans, strengthen your security system where necessary.

05

Central Monitoring System

24/7 centralised security monitoring with real-time surveillance, rapid incident detection, alert management and coordinated response to ensure continuous protection of your people, property and assets.

06

Bodyguard

Our professional bodyguards provide close protection for individuals, executives, and VIPs, ensuring their safety in every situation.

07

Residential & Commercial Security

We offer tailored security solutions for homes, businesses, and commercial properties to ensure a safe and secure environment. Our services include manned guarding, access control, and routine inspection to prevent risks and respond swiftly.

08

Access Control Management

We implement advanced access control systems to manage and monitor entry points, ensuring authorised access only. Our solutions include keycard systems, biometric access, visitor management, and integrated security platforms.

09

CCTV Monitoring & Surveillance

Our surveillance solutions provide 24/7 real-time monitoring and recording to detect, deter, and respond to incidents. High-definition CCTV systems are installed strategically and monitored by our trained operators.

10

Event & Special Assignment

We provide professional security for events, gatherings, and special assignments with precision and discretion. Our team is experienced in crowd management, access control, VIP protection, and emergency preparedness.

11

Patrol & Emergency Response

Our rapid response team and patrol units are always ready to respond to emergencies and ensure immediate action. We conduct mobile patrols, incident response, and coordination with relevant authorities for maximum safety.
